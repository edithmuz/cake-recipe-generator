AI Cake Recipe Generator

Cakes are generally categorized by their texture and leavening method, primarily falling into butter (shortened) cakes, foam (sponge) cakes, and chiffon cakes.
Popular types include rich pound cake, airy angel food cake, velvety red velvet, decadent chocolate, and tangy cheesecake, often enhanced with fillings like ganache, buttercream, or fruit conserve.
